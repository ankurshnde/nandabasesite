# Youth Chapter

The NANDA Youth Chapter supports university students, academic fellows, and early-stage researchers working on distributed multi-agent systems and civic artificial intelligence.

---

## Fellowship Programs

### 1. Student Research Fellowship
A competitive six-month remote fellowship pairing students with core contributors to author peer-reviewed preprints and engineering artifacts. Fellows receive compute credits, access to simulation testbeds, and publication support.

### 2. University AI Lab Ambassador Program
Campus ambassadors organize local study groups, hackathons, and guest lectures on distributed systems and agentic AI.

### 3. Civic Engineering Micro-Grants
Direct compute and infrastructure funding ($500 to $5,000) for student engineering teams building open-source public service agents for their local communities.

---

## Eligibility and Application

- Open to undergraduate, master's, and doctoral candidates enrolled at accredited universities.
- Applicants must demonstrate proficiency in Python, Rust, or C++ and familiarity with distributed computing or machine learning fundamentals.
- Applications for the 2026-2027 cohort open in October 2026.
