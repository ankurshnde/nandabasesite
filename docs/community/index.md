# Community Overview

The NANDA community is composed of researchers, software engineers, public policy specialists, and students collaborating on open-source autonomous agent architectures.

---

## Structure of the Community

### Working Groups
Working groups focus on core technical and policy domain areas:
- **Protocol Engineering**: Mesh synchronization, gossip protocols, and network topology.
- **Safety and Alignment**: Formal methods, cryptographic action verification, and threat modeling.
- **Civic Applications**: Municipal workflows, vernacular language datasets, and field deployment logistics.

### Regional Chapters
Local chapters operate in various regions to organize technical meetups, localized data collection, and university partnerships. See [Global Chapters](global-chapter.md) for regional contact points.

### Academic and Student Fellowships
The [Youth Chapter](youth-chapter.md) coordinates student research fellowships, compute grants, and mentorship programs with senior contributors.

---

## Communication Channels

- **Developer Forum & RFCs**: Hosted on GitHub Discussions for architectural change proposals.
- **Community Meetings**: Bi-weekly technical working sessions held every alternate Thursday at 17:00 UTC.
- **Documentation Contributions**: Community members can submit improvements directly to the documentation repository.
