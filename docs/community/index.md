## How Can You Join?

* **Start or Join a Chapter**: Launch a local hub or connect with regional organizers on the [Start a Chapter!](start-a-chapter.md) page.
* **Student Ambassadors**: Become a student ambassador by joining the youth chapter at [NANDA Shapers](https://www.nandashapers.org/).
* **Research Groups**: Join active paper-writing labs and collaborative research teams via the [Get Involved](../publication/get-involved.md) page.
* **Event Calendar**: Subscribe to upcoming community webinars, hackathons, and talks on the [Luma Calendar](https://lu.ma/nanda).
* **Open Source Contribution**: Build reference implementations, adapters, and protocols via [Reference Implementation](../developer/reference-implementation.md) and [Open Source](../developer/open-source.md) repositories.
