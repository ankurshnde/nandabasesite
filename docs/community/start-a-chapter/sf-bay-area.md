# Start a Chapter! {: .nanda-main-title }

A chapter is the local node of a single global network. It is not a franchise and not a separate project.

Local chapters serve as interconnected nodes in a worldwide network, building on Project NANDA's vision to architect the open agentic web in the local environment through meetups, open-source work, startup and university connections, and policy conversations.

Chapters promote the same open-source projects and shared technologies without inventing an isolated local stack. A chapter maintains an active public presence, stays closely connected to other chapters across the globe, and ensures that all public material it produces remains open and hosted by FAN.

---

## Project NANDA Innovation Council

The **Innovation Council** constitutes the leadership core of a local chapter.

Council leaders run the chapter, while council members collaborate actively with them. Together, they are the individuals who keep the local group tied to the broader global network: convening community events, driving open-source development, engaging startups and research universities, and stewarding the local side of public policy.

If you want to start a chapter in a city that is not currently listed below, you register for the Innovation Council first. That formal registration is what officially charters the new group as an authorized chapter of Project NANDA.

To register, email [contact@projectnanda.org](mailto:contact@projectnanda.org).

## Benefits for Leaders

* **Early Adopter Advantage**: Pioneer the next wave of decentralized AI innovation and cultivate deep architectural expertise in multi-agent networks — a vital capability for the coming decade.
* **Research & University Network**: Join a collaborative network that includes 15+ pioneering AI universities, participating in papers, specifications, and research previews before public release.
* **Venture & Startup Access**: Direct access to founders, builders, and VC ecosystems building atop Project NANDA foundations.
* **Commercial Pathways**: Gain early strategic insight into commercialization pathways, deployment architectures, and emerging market opportunities.
* **Organizational Alignment**: Connect your institution or company directly with cutting-edge agentic AI trends and cross-sector partnerships.

## Benefits for Members

* **Hands-On Workshops**: Participate in technical deep-dives, protocol sandboxes, research reviews, and collaborative hackathons.
* **Expert Mentorship**: Direct knowledge exchange and guidance from leading decentralized AI researchers and core engineers.
* **Speaking Opportunities**: Represent Project NANDA and take the stage at local meetups, conferences, and technical panels.
* **Pioneer Recognition**: Receive official recognition as a pioneer member within the global open agentic web ecosystem.

## How to Start a Chapter

Ready to bring Project NANDA to your metropolitan area? Follow this sequential process:

1. **Verify City Availability**: First confirm that your city or region is not already in the existing chapter directory above. If an active chapter exists, join that local community.
2. **Register for the Innovation Council**: If your city is not yet established, send an email to [contact@projectnanda.org](mailto:contact@projectnanda.org) requesting Innovation Council registration. Please include your city name, background, and your goals for convening developers, researchers, startups, and civic partners.
3. **Official Charter & Launch**: Wait for written confirmation and charter review before announcing a chapter, naming a public group after Project NANDA, or presenting yourself as the local lead.

  <strong>Student & University Chapters</strong>: Campus-based student groups and academic ambassador programs are chartered separately through <a href="https://www.nandashapers.org/">NANDA Shapers</a>.
