# Global Chapters

NANDA Chapters coordinate localized open-source activities, research workshops, and deployment partnerships across regional jurisdictions.

---

## Active Chapters

### 1. NANDA South Asia
- **Focus Areas**: Indic language model fine-tuning, high-density pilgrim crowd logistics (KumbhDoot), and rural governance interfaces.
- **Coordination**: Regional Working Committee
- **Contact**: `south-asia@nanda.ai`

---

### 2. NANDA Europe
- **Focus Areas**: Regulatory compliance frameworks, GDPR-aligned zero-knowledge attestation, and smart municipal resource monitoring.
- **Coordination**: European Systems Group
- **Contact**: `europe@nanda.ai`

---

### 3. NANDA Americas
- **Focus Areas**: Distributed execution engines, formal verification tooling, and university laboratory research collaborations.
- **Coordination**: North American Working Group
- **Contact**: `americas@nanda.ai`

---

### 4. NANDA Africa
- **Focus Areas**: Offline-first mesh computing, regional dialect speech pipelines, and agricultural resource coordination.
- **Coordination**: African Regional Council
- **Contact**: `africa@nanda.ai`

---

## Establishing a Chapter

New chapters must be chartered through the Community Working Group by submitting an operational plan covering:
1. Minimum of three principal organizers from different institutions or organizations.
2. An annual schedule of open technical sessions.
3. Explicit adherence to open-source governance guidelines.
