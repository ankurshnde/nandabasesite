# Hackathons

NANDA Hackathons bring together developers, researchers, and builders from across the globe to advance the Internet of AI Agents. Participants design open-source protocols, build sandboxes, and create services that autonomous agents can discover and use independently.

---

## Upcoming Hackathons

We are currently planning our upcoming hackathons with partners from academic, developer, and venture ecosystems.

If you are interested in hosting, sponsoring, or partnering for a local or global NANDA hackathon, reach out to us at [contact@projectnanda.org](mailto:contact@projectnanda.org).
