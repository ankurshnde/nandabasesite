# Hackathons

NANDA hackathons bring together researchers, builders, students, and professionals to design, build, and evaluate services that autonomous AI agents can discover, invoke, and coordinate with independently.

---

## NandaHack: Agentic AI Hackathon

**Hosted by MIT Media Lab & HCLTech**

- **Format:** Hybrid (Compete online or in-person at MIT Media Lab)
- **Eligibility:** Students, builders, researchers, and professionals (solo or teams)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Focus:** Build services that autonomous AI agents can discover and use independently.

### Links & Registration
- [Apply on Google Form](https://forms.gle/HKCSitSChcFSqyzY8)
- [Hackathon Website](https://nandahack.media.mit.edu/)
- [Explore NANDA Town](https://nandatown.projectnanda.org/)

---

## How It Works

1. **Warm-up**: Improve one of the building blocks in NANDA Town, or add a new one.
2. **Main Event**: Host a service online with a verifiable descriptor or SKILL.md so AI agents can use it autonomously.
