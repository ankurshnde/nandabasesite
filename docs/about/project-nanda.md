# About Project NANDA

Project NANDA is an open initiative dedicated to building the foundational infrastructure for the Internet of AI Agents.

---

## Origin & Mission

Project NANDA originated as research at the **MIT Media Lab** and continues to draw on foundational research from academic and industry partners worldwide.

As autonomous AI agents become pervasive across organizations, domains, and cloud environments, they face a critical coordination challenge:
> *How can billions of AI agents discover each other, verify capabilities, and coordinate tasks without central gatekeepers, bottlenecks, or security vulnerabilities?*

Project NANDA addresses this challenge by providing minimal, open, federated infrastructure layers:
- **Identity & Discovery**: The federated **NANDA Index** and **AgentFacts** verifiable capability records.
- **Interoperability**: Adapters and protocols allowing agents built on different frameworks (LangChain, CrewAI, MCP, A2A) to communicate.
- **Evaluation & Sandboxes**: **NEST** and **Nanda Town** for realistic multi-agent simulation and testing.

---

## Institutional Home

Project NANDA sits within the **Foundation for Agentic Networks (FAN)** ([agenticnet.org](https://www.agenticnet.org/)), providing a neutral, public-benefit, standards-oriented home for open-agent-network infrastructure.
