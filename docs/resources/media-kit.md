# Media Kit

This page provides official brand assets, approved typography specifications, and institutional descriptions for public reporting and publications.

---

## Institutional Summary

> **Institutional Overview**:  
> NANDA is an open-source research initiative developing decentralized, autonomous multi-agent architectures for public infrastructure, crowd safety, and civic intelligence. Through standardized benchmarking suites (NANDA Index), simulation environments (NEST & NANDA Town), and empirical field deployments (KumbhDoot & DigitDoot), NANDA provides foundational protocols and tooling for reliable autonomous systems.

## Brand Typography & Palette

- **Primary Typeface**: Inter (UI and Headings)
- **Monospace Typeface**: JetBrains Mono (Code, Metrics, and Technical References)
- **Primary Contrast Color**: Slate Black (`#111827` / `#0D1117`)
- **Neutral Accent**: Cool Gray (`#4B5563` / `#8B949E`)
- **Border Tone**: Minimal Gray (`#E5E7EB` / `#30363D`)
