# Publications

<div class="nanda-pub-subtitle">Shared science, technical specifications, and research manuscripts from Project NANDA</div>

Project NANDA’s publications span the three phases of its development roadmap: **Agent Foundation**, **Agent Commerce**, and **Agent Society**.

Additional sections cover decentralized-AI philosophy, multistakeholder governance, civic applications, and related research methods that inform the program across organizational boundaries.
